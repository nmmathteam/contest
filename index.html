Who are we?

The New Mexico Math Team (NMMT) are the organizers of the New Mexico Math Meet.

Many of our members have qualified for the AIME, MATHCOUNTS Nationals, and have placed in UNM-PNM.

We teach and learn from each other to prepare for and compete in various team-based math competitions.

We have carefully written each problem in order to provide an accessible and high-quality competition.

About the contest

The New Mexico Math Meet (NM³) is an in-person math competition for middle school students across the state of New Mexico. We aim to provide a high-quality, challenging, accessible competition to motivated students interested in mathematics.
