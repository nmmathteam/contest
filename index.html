What is NM³?

The New Mexico Math Meet (NM³) is an in-person team-based math competition for New Mexico middle school students.

Learn more

NM³ is a fun team contest open to middle schoolers of all levels. We focus on creativity, collaboration, and a positive environment for young math enthusiasts.

The contest features a mix of team rounds that test problem solving ingenuity, collaboration, and critical thinking. Every round is designed to be fun and rewarding!

Our questions are written, selected, and reviewed by experienced high school students to ensure they are clear and challenging while remaining accessible.

About NMMT

The New Mexico Math Team (NMMT) are the organizers of the New Mexico Math Meet. We teach and learn from each other to prepare for and compete in various team-based math competitions. Many of our members have qualified for the AIME, MATHCOUNTS Nationals, and have placed in UNM-PNM!
